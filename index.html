§01

Introduction

Most machine-learning projects die in a notebook. Mine are built to leave it.

I'm Shre Hari — an MSc Artificial Intelligence candidate at the University of Sheffield with a particular obsession: the unglamorous gap between a promising model and a system people actually rely on. I work both sides of that gap — currently as an AI Engineer Intern at NexCell Solutions, building the backend of ConneX, a live AI receptionist and CRM platform for UK letting agencies; previously engineering production backends at Hamiltonian Systems that moved 100,000+ records a week; and writing peer-reviewed research on real-time disease monitoring, accepted at the 2nd IEEE International Conference on Circuits, Power and Intelligent Systems (CCPIS 2025).

My favourite results rarely come from swapping in a bigger model. They come from structured error analysis — like discovering that intra-sentence boundary truncation, not model capacity, was throttling an extractive QA system, and lifting test-set F1 from 0.50 to 0.69 with targeted scoring adjustments. Diagnosis before replacement. Evidence before opinion.

I'm completing my MSc in Sheffield this September — deep learning, NLP, speech & audio processing, and scalable systems — and available immediately, full-time, for a team whose hard problems deserve that kind of stubbornness.

Experiments

Ten systems. Every claim below shipped with a number attached.

EXP.08 2026 MSC DISSERTATION · RESULTS SEP 2026

Visualising Emotion — Can a Text-to-Image Model Draw a Feeling?

1,012 public self-reports of emotion (anonymised, names redacted), typed at a Sheffield engagement event and rendered by DALL·E as colour-free line drawings. Rather than forcing a vision model to guess feelings, the pipeline describes each image in neutral terms (BLIP visual-question answering), reads the descriptions with zero-shot NLI, extracts prompt emotion with GoEmotions, and scores prompt–image agreement on a ladder of metrics against a 300-shuffle permutation null.

⤷ Method result: describe-then-extract doubles emotion-family agreement over direct VLM classification. Human-rater validation drafted; final results September 2026.

  • GoEmotions
  • BLIP · Zero-shot NLI
  • CLIP
  • Permutation Testing

EXP.02 2025

Extractive Document QA System

End-to-end QA pipeline on roberta-base-squad2: 300-word overlapping chunks and a custom composite score blending model confidence, span-length reward and position-aware penalties. The win came from error analysis, not model swaps — intra-sentence boundary truncation was the primary failure mode.

0.50 → 0.69 TEST-SET F1 (+38% REL.)

EXP.03 2025

Speech Rate Classification System

Task-specific handcrafted features over log-mel filterbank energies — 128-dim spectral statistics for speed, 256-dim temporal deltas for tempo — matched to each task's acoustic physics. SVM with RBF kernel, 3-fold grid-search CV.

98.2% SPEAKING-SPEED ACCURACY · kNN BASELINE 24.6%

Medical Image Clustering — BloodMNIST

Compressed 12,288-feature blood-cell images to 32 dimensions two ways: PCA versus a convolutional autoencoder (MSE loss converging to 0.005 over 40 epochs). K-Means (k=8) over the learned latent space, validated with t-SNE structure plots across all 8 cell categories.
